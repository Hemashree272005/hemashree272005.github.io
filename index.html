RL-Driven Autonomous Rover for Hazard Detection & Environmental Exploration

2026
Team project · 3 members

A simulated rover that learns to navigate a disaster-hit area and map the full space — with no hazard map handed to it in advance. Hazards are discovered through perception, not given as ground truth.

  • Tabular Q-learning over a discretized grid, paired with a lightweight CNN hazard classifier trained on synthetic imagery.
  • Ran a 4-way reward ablation (baseline / coverage / curiosity / full risk-aware) to confirm the coverage-vs-safety trade-off the team was targeting.
  • Built a step-by-step visual demo of the rover scanning cells, updating its hazard map, and choosing its next move.

IoT-Enabled Smart Walker with Sensor-Fusion Fall Risk Prediction and Adaptive Mobility Assistance

Co-Inventor, with Ponn Oviyaa S · Faculty Guide: Dr. Subitha D · Inventive disclosure filed with IIPRD

An Arduino-based smart walker for elderly mobility support that predicts fall risk before it happens, instead of only reacting after a fall.

  • Dual MPU6050 IMUs and dual HX711 load cells give bilateral fall detection and real-time weight-asymmetry monitoring.
  • A sensor-fusion Fall Risk Prediction engine and a Dynamic Stability Reserve score combine tilt, weight imbalance, and obstacle proximity to flag deteriorating stability early.
  • Runs entirely standalone — no cloud, no smartphone dependency — at an estimated hardware cost under USD 30.
